01 // Core Principle

THE MARKET ALWAYS LIES

The Fundamental Inversion

The market always feels strongest near the top and weakest near the bottom. Euphoria is not a signal to enter. It is a signal to wait. Discomfort is not a signal to exit. It is a signal to accumulate.

I do not buy because prices are rising.
I do not buy because influencers are excited.
I do not buy because everyone believes the future is obvious.
I buy when uncertainty is high, sentiment is weak, and fear dominates.

Action

Reasoning

NEUTRAL
Action
Observe. Continue saving cash. Continue research. No significant deployment unless a specific asset trades at an exceptional valuation.
Reasoning
This is the waiting zone. Most investors become impatient here. Patience is not passivity. It is precision held in reserve.
50

GREED
Action
No new major positions. Maintain existing positions only. Any desire to increase exposure requires written justification and a 48-hour waiting period.
Reasoning
Opportunity cost feels painful here. That pain is the protocol working. Emotional mistakes are most common in this zone.
74

EXTREME GREED
Action
No buying. If urgency to buy is felt, do nothing for 72 hours. The feeling itself is the warning signal.
Reasoning
This is historically where the largest volume of emotional decision-making occurs. My job is not to participate in euphoria. My job is to preserve capital for future opportunity.

03 // Psychological Checkpoint

BEFORE EVERY PURCHASE

"Am I buying because this asset is attractive, or because I am afraid of missing out?"
If the answer is fear of missing out:
Do not buy.
Wait 48 hours.
Return to the question with a clear record and a clear mind.
04 // The Long-Term Reminder

INVESTING IS WAITING

On Time
Months may pass. Years may pass. That is not failure. That is the protocol operating exactly as intended.
On Idle Capital
Capital may sit without deployment. That is not weakness. That is preserved optionality waiting for asymmetric conditions.
On Constant Action
The objective is not to always be invested. The objective is to deploy capital when risk and reward become asymmetrical.
On Opportunity
If the index does not justify deployment, hold cash without guilt. The market will provide another opportunity. It always does.
